OUR HERITAGE

Historical Journey & Board of Directors

To establish deep institutional trust, we celebrate a multi-decade legacy of execution, governance, and industrial leadership.

1979

The Beginning

Vijay Kumar Gupta entered the business world under the guidance of his father, Shri Ramswaroop Laharia Ji. With a strong desire to understand the intricacies of business, he embarked on a journey of entrepreneurship, laying the foundation for a lasting business legacy.

1981 - 1985

Market Penetration & Expansion

The journey began in Kanpur, where Vijay Kumar Gupta personally spearheaded the marketing efforts through his dedication and perseverance. He travelled extensively, covering every street and locality to ensure comprehensive market penetration. After successfully establishing the distribution of edible oil and vanaspati ghee products among retailers across Kanpur, he expanded operations throughout Uttar Pradesh. Through consistent efforts and meticulous market development, he ultimately achieved comprehensive coverage across the entire state—mapping the market not only area-wise but also retailer-by-retailer.

1985

Establishment of VIJAY BROTHERS

He established VIJAY BROTHERS as a proprietorship business engaged in merchant trading and order supply — marking an important step in his entrepreneurial journey and establishing his independent footprint in the trade sector.

1997 - 2002

Entry into Manufacturing — A.V. Agro Products Pvt. Ltd. as a Founder Director

With a vision to enter the manufacturing sector, Vijay Kumar Gupta became the Founder-Director of A.V. Agro Products Private Limited and established an edible oil refinery.

2004

Acquisition & Expansion — Vaibhav Edibles Private Limited

Acquired Vaibhav Edibles Private Limited and expanded its production capacity of Edible Oils, strengthening the group's presence in the edible oil industry.

2006

Entry of Vishal Gupta - Engineering Modernisation

Vishal Gupta joined the organisation as an Engineering Specialist. Leveraging his technical expertise and strategic vision, he played a pivotal role in modernising the plant and creating a strong foundation for future expansion.

2009

Brand Launch — Entry into Marketing

The group decided to enter the marketing sector and launched the JIOLITE Brand — marking a major milestone in establishing a direct, consumer-focused presence.

2014–16

Resilience & Transformation — Rise from the Ashes

On April 25, 2014, the Vaibhav Edibles factory was devastated by a massive fire that completely destroyed the plant. Despite this setback, the organisation demonstrated exceptional resilience. In 2015–16, construction of a new, modern plant with twice the production capacity was initiated — turning a major challenge into an opportunity for transformation and growth.

2018

Sonam Vishal Gupta — Financial & Institutional Strengthening

Sonam Vishal Gupta brought her expertise to strengthen the organisation's institutional framework, organisational systems, and financial administration — contributing to a more structured, disciplined, and sustainable approach to growth.

2024

45-Year Legacy & JPPL Incorporation

After more than 45 years of entrepreneurial experience, execution, and industry expertise, the leadership team continued to drive expansion across Animal Nutrition, FMCG, and Business & Digital Services. On 11 April 2024, this rich legacy entered a new chapter with the official incorporation of Jiolite Products Private Limited. Under the leadership of Vijay Kumar Gupta and Vishal Gupta, the organisation is committed to driving the next phase of growth through technology, IT solutions, and digital marketing.

Today

Launch of Lahariya Solution and Services

Building on a foundation of business experience and regulatory expertise, the group has launched Lahariya Solution and Services — an integrated, nationwide service platform delivering practical IT and business solutions across India.
