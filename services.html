Category A: Legal Setup & Registrations

Formal legal establishment, tax infrastructure setups, and brand safeguarding certifications for entities operating across states.

1. GST Registration

Overview

Simplify tax compliance. We handle comprehensive registration for Proprietorships, Partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies, OPCs, and E-commerce sellers.

Client Value & Statutory Benefits

Grants you a formal legal identity, unlocks Input Tax Credit (ITC), allows pan-India commerce, and qualifies you for Government Tenders and GeM registrations.

Required Checklist / Documents:

PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Business Address Proof (Rent Agreement/Utility Bill), Photo, and Bank Details.

2. MSME / Udyam Registration

Overview

Official government recognition for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (applicable for Manufacturers, Service Providers, Traders, and Startups).

Client Value & Statutory Benefits

Unlocks massive government subsidy benefits, lower interest rates on business bank loans, priority sector lending, and faster processing of industrial approvals.

Processing Timeline:

Guaranteed 1-Day processing from submission.

Read Detailed Guide & Documents

3. FSSAI Registration

Overview

Mandatory Food Safety License issued by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India for restaurants, hotels, cloud kitchens, grocery traders, and food packers.

Client Value & Statutory Benefits

Establishes complete legal compliance, builds consumer trust, avoids hefty municipal penalties, and is strictly required to onboard onto online platforms (Zomato/Swiggy).

Requirements:

Business address proof, identity proof, list of food categories, and layout map of the manufacturing site (for manufacturing licenses).

Read Detailed Guide & Documents

4. IEC (Import Export Code)

Overview

A mandatory 10-digit business identity code issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) for any entity engaging in cross-border trade.

Client Value & Statutory Benefits

Grants you global marketplace access, enables seamless import/export operations, and allows your firm to claim government export incentive schemes.

Checklist:

PAN Card of the entity, bank certificate or canceled check, address proof of business.

Read Detailed Guide & Documents

5. Trademark Registration (TM / ®)

Overview

Long-term brand protection that legally safeguards your unique brand name, logo, design symbols, or corporate tagline.

Client Value & Statutory Benefits

Delivers exclusive legal ownership, prevents copycats from diluting your reputation, and builds intangible business equity over its 10-year renewable validity.

Checklist:

Logo/brand name copy, identity proof of applicant, Power of Attorney authorization document.
